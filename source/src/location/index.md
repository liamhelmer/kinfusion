# Location

## Rhizome Springs, Saltspring Island BC

Kin-Fusion Campout 2026 takes place at [Rhizome Springs](https://www.rhizomesprings.com/), a stunning retreat and gathering space nestled in the natural beauty of Saltspring Island, British Columbia.

## Getting There

Saltspring Island is accessible by BC Ferries from Vancouver (Tsawwassen → Long Harbour) and from Victoria (Swartz Bay → Fulford Harbour). The island is also reachable by floatplane from Vancouver or Seattle.

More detailed travel information will be posted as the event approaches.

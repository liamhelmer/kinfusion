# Schedule

The full schedule for Kin-Fusion Campout 2026 will be posted closer to the event.

## Overview

**Thursday, September 10** — Optional early arrival. Settle in, meet your fellow participants, evening social.

**Friday, September 11** — Main arrival day. Orientation, evening dance.

**Saturday, September 12** — Full day: morning workshops, afternoon unconference sessions, evening dance.

**Sunday, September 13** — Morning sessions, afternoon wind-down, closing circle, evening gathering. Event ends Sunday night.

**Monday, September 14** — Optional late departure for those staying the extra night.

## Unconference Sessions

A big part of Kin-Fusion is the unconference — participant-led sessions proposed and scheduled by attendees. You can [propose a workshop or session](/unconference/) when you register. The schedule is built collaboratively on-site.

*Detailed timing will be shared with confirmed participants closer to the event.*

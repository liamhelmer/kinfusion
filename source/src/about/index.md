# About Kin-Fusion Campout

Kin-Fusion is a community-run, not-for-profit fusion dance unconference. We gather about 90 people for three days of dance, music, workshops, and shared meals in the beautiful natural setting of Rhizome Springs on Saltspring Island, BC.

## What to Expect

- **Fusion dance** — a space for partner dancing that blends styles and invites playful exploration
- **Community DJs** — dancing into the night with music curated by people who love to dance
- **Unconference sessions** — participant-led workshops, discussions, and skill-shares (you propose them!)
- **Shared meals** — food is provided for all participants
- **Co-creation** — everyone is asked to contribute in small ways to help the event run smoothly

## Dates & Pricing

**Dates:** Arrive Thursday September 10 or Friday September 11, 2026. Event runs September 11–13. Optional Monday September 14 departure.

**Sliding scale pricing:**
- $300 — base tier (makes the event possible)
- $350 — supporter tier (helps us offer scholarships)
- $400 — sustainer tier (makes the event better for everyone)

Higher tiers support partial scholarships for those in need. [Scholarships are available](/faq/) — no questions asked.

**Kids:** Children 12 and under attend free. Older children pay the regular price. We're working to provide a dedicated quiet space and child-friendly activities.

## Registration

This is a **soft-cap application model** — we review applications and confirm participants by email. Payment is made by Interac e-transfer or Wyse to Rhizome Springs after acceptance. [Apply to attend &rarr;](/register/)

## The Venue

[Rhizome Springs](https://www.rhizomesprings.com/) is a retreat and gathering space on Saltspring Island, BC — nestled in nature with space for dancing, camping, and community life.

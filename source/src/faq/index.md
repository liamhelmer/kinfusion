# Frequently Asked Questions

## Registration & Pricing

**How does registration work?**
Kin-Fusion uses a soft-cap application model. You submit an application and we review it. Accepted participants receive a confirmation email with payment instructions. Payment is made via Interac e-transfer or Wyse to Rhizome Springs.

**What are the ticket prices?**
We use a sliding scale:
- **$300** — Base tier: makes the event possible
- **$350** — Supporter tier: helps fund scholarships
- **$400** — Sustainer tier: makes the event better for everyone

Choose the amount that reflects your means. Higher tiers help us make the event accessible to more people.

**Are scholarships available?**
Yes. Partial scholarships are available for those who need them. Just mention it in your registration application — no detailed explanation required.

**Can kids come?**
Absolutely. Kin-Fusion is family-friendly. **Children 12 and under attend free.** Older children pay the regular sliding-scale price. We're working to provide a dedicated quiet space and child-friendly unconference activities.

## The Event

**What is fusion dance?**
Fusion dance is a form of partnered improvised dance that draws from many styles — blues, swing, tango, contact improv, and more. It's playful, creative, and welcoming to dancers at all levels.

**Do I need to know how to fusion dance?**
Newcomers are welcome. Kin-Fusion is a community event, not a competition. There will be space for learning and exploration.

**What's an unconference?**
An unconference is a participant-led conference where the schedule is created by the people who show up. Anyone can propose a session — a workshop, a discussion, a skill-share. The schedule is built collaboratively on-site.

**Is food provided?**
Yes, food is provided for all participants during the event.

**What should I bring?**
Packing details will be shared with confirmed participants. General guidelines: comfortable clothes for dancing, layers for evenings, and anything you need for camping or shared accommodation.

## Logistics

**How do I get to Saltspring Island?**
Saltspring Island is accessible by BC Ferries from Vancouver (Tsawwassen → Long Harbour) and Victoria (Swartz Bay → Fulford Harbour), and by floatplane. More details will be shared closer to the event.

**Can I arrive early or leave late?**
Early arrival is welcome on Thursday, September 10. You're also welcome to stay until Monday morning, September 14, if you wish.
